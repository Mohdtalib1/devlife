DevLife: Account & Data Deletion

Compliant with Google Play User Data Policy

DevLife provides users with the ability to delete their account and associated data. You can submit an account and data deletion request using the email template below.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Firebase Authentication Account: Your registered Google Account email address and unique Firebase User Identifier (UID) are permanently deleted from Firebase Authentication.
  • Subscription & RevenueCat Records: Your linked customer identifier is unlinked and purged from active subscription management records.
  • Local Device Saves: Uninstalling DevLife or clearing app data in your device settings permanently removes all local game saves, career progression, and simulated currency from your device.

Note: DevLife does not collect, register, or store any separate username on our servers. Your authentication is tied solely to your Google email address. Any in-game character name chosen during play is stored only locally on your phone.

Data Retention Policy

Historical financial transaction records (such as order IDs and payment receipts processed through Google Play Billing) may be retained as required by applicable tax and legal compliance obligations.

Active Subscriptions Notice:

Deleting your DevLife account or uninstalling the app does NOT cancel recurring subscriptions in Google Play. To prevent Google from auto-billing future renewal cycles, cancel active subscriptions prior to account deletion via Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Submit Account & Data Deletion Request

To request permanent deletion of your account and associated data, please send an email to the developer at mtq751@gmail.com.

Send Account Deletion Email

Account deletion requests received by email are verified and processed within 30 days. Once deleted, all cloud-linked purchases and account data cannot be recovered.
