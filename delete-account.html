In compliance with the Google Play User Data Policy (Account Deletion Requirement), DevJourney (developed by Mk Sikander, account owner Mohd Kafeel) provides all users with a direct, web-based pathway to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal and gameplay data.

You can submit an account deletion request directly through this web page at any time—without needing to reinstall or access the app.

Steps to Request Account & Data Deletion

  1. Provide your registered Google Sign-In email: Enter the exact Google email address you used to sign into DevJourney.
  2. Submit your request: Use the web submission form below to send your deletion request to the developer (mtq751@gmail.com).
  3. Verification & Deletion Processing: Our team verifies your email against Firebase Authentication records and permanently deletes your account and data within 30 days. You will receive an email confirmation once completed.

Submit Deletion Request

Fill in your details below to submit your permanent account and data deletion request directly to the developer.

What Data Is Permanently Deleted

  • Firebase Authentication Record: Your Google Account email address and unique Firebase User Identifier (UID) are permanently deleted from Firebase Authentication.
  • Entitlement & Subscription Identifier: Your customer record in RevenueCat is purged and unlinked from your user ID.
  • Game Progression & Saved Data: All associated career progress, decisions, milestones, and virtual statistics are completely purged. Uninstalling the app also removes all local cache and database saves from your device.

What Data Is Retained & Legal Justification

Financial & Statutory Records: Transaction identifiers, order IDs, and billing receipts processed through Google Play Billing may be retained solely to the extent required by applicable financial, taxation, anti-fraud, and statutory compliance laws. We do not store credit card or payment credential numbers.

Important Notice for Active Subscriptions:

Deleting your DevJourney account or uninstalling the app does NOT cancel recurring subscriptions processed by Google Play. To prevent Google Play from automatically billing upcoming cycles, please cancel any active subscriptions before or after requesting account deletion via: Google Play Store app → Profile Icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → DevJourney → Cancel subscription.
