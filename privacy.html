Privacy Policy for DevLife

Last updated: September 2026

DevLife is an independently developed mobile game created and maintained by an individual developer ("developer", "I", or "we"). This Privacy Policy details how information is handled, processed, and protected when you download and play DevLife.

Summary: Privacy is taken seriously. DevLife does not collect advertising IDs, track your location, or transmit your in-game career simulation to any remote servers. Data processing is strictly limited to account authentication (Google Sign-In via Firebase) and in-app purchase verification (RevenueCat). There are no separate usernames or passwords stored on any server.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal data required to provide application functionality:

  • Account Information: Account creation is required to play DevLife. When you sign in with Google, authentication is handled securely via Firebase Authentication. We receive only your Google Account email address and a system-generated Firebase User Identifier (UID) to authenticate your access, keep your session active, and link purchases across devices. We do not create, collect, or store any separate username or password on our servers. Any in-game character name you choose during gameplay is saved solely on your local device.
  • Purchase History & Subscription Data: When you purchase subscriptions or packages, transaction metadata (such as order IDs, active entitlement status, and product IDs) is verified via Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. We never collect or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or banking credentials.
  • Device & Diagnostic Identifiers: Anonymous installation tokens and operational diagnostics (e.g., authentication latency and network request error codes) handled automatically by Firebase and RevenueCat SDKs to maintain app reliability and combat fraud.
  • In-Game Simulation Data: All game decisions, skills, career arcs, storylines, and simulated finances are stored strictly on your local device storage. They are never uploaded to any cloud backend or shared with third parties.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for the following operational purposes:

  • Authenticating your user session across app restarts.
  • Granting and syncing entitled in-app purchases and subscriptions across devices.
  • Preventing fraudulent access and diagnosing technical system failures.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted industry service providers who process data strictly on our behalf:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google LLC): Identity and authentication infrastructure.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: In-app purchase verification and entitlement management.
  • Google Play Billing (Google LLC): Secure payment and subscription processing.

4. Data Sharing and Selling

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information. We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, ad networks, or data brokers.

5. Security & Encryption in Transit

All network data transmitted by DevLife to our service providers (Firebase and RevenueCat) is strictly encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS 1.2+ protocols.

6. Account & Data Deletion Rights

You have the full right to delete your account and request complete erasure of your associated data at any time. To submit an account and data deletion request, please visit our Account & Data Deletion Portal or email us at mtq751@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

DevLife does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where required by local law). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

8. Contact Us

For questions or privacy concerns regarding this policy, please reach out to us at: mtq751@gmail.com.
